About vendor stock and maps

Vendor items and coordinates are historical Classic references, unverified on Forever. Map backgrounds come from the Forever beta client, while markers use Classic coordinates. Restock comments report waits rather than server timers and can conflict. Missing reports mean unknown waits. X/Y are percentages (0–100), not GPS.
