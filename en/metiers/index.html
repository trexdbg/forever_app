Trainer sources and map accuracy

Profession trainers include names from Forever-oriented guides and Zephras Isle waypoints, while weapon masters and many positions come from Classic guides. Markers and training availability are not confirmed in-game. Trainers without reliable coordinates appear in the directory without an invented marker or /way command. Map backgrounds come from the Forever beta client; X/Y are zone percentages (0–100), not real-world GPS.
