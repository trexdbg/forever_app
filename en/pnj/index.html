About the data and respawn times

Vendor and profession trainer locations are historical World of Warcraft Classic references. WoW Forever may differ. Map backgrounds are from the Forever beta client; plotted NPC locations remain Classic references. Item and NPC links open Wowhead Classic or Forever. Comment reports describe observed waiting times rather than measured server restock intervals: reports may come from different versions of the game and may conflict. Missing evidence means unknown, not immediate respawn. X/Y are zone percentages (0–100), not world GPS coordinates.
