The Stockade in Forever: boss loot and beta changes

Check reported changes without confusing Classic loot with Forever beta drops.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

What changed in The Stockade?

This returning Classic dungeon has a reference level bracket of 24–32 in Stormwind City. Our local beta references list the following changes:

  • Forever adds Rare-quality items to several bosses; some Classic rare encounters remain reference-only.
  • Forever Group Finder client data caps the Stockade at five players versus ten in Classic Era. In-game drop reports are still too sparse for reliable rates.

These are attributed beta sources, not guarantees that every listed reward currently drops.
