Humar the Pridelord in WoW Forever: location and loot

Reference guide to Humar the Pridelord's drops and coordinates.

Editorial review: 23 October 2026

Where can I find Humar the Pridelord?

Humar the Pridelord is a level 23 rare elite in The Barrens. Community Forever guides give the waypoint 61.0 / 35.0; our earlier 62.0 / 33.3 point remains a Classic reference. The documented community point is a search lead, not a live spawn confirmation.

How long is its respawn?

The available historical interval is 8–12 h. That is not a confirmed timer for a Forever realm. Check all possible points instead of relying on just one spawn location.

What if the rare is missing from the reference coordinates?

Humar may not be standing at the first X/Y mark at the time you arrive. The rare atlas can show additional documented map points; check nearby movement and patrol areas rather than assuming a failed spawn. The reported Mark of the Pack Leader is a loot reference, not a guaranteed kill reward. Neither an old respawn window nor a community loot report proves the rules of the current Forever realm.
