How do you filter?

In BiS by level, select class and the exact level-30 bracket. The character paper-doll separates slots and prevents invalid armor selection, such as mail or plate for a rogue. Item level is different from required level.

What about levels 31–60?

Those are future planning brackets, but gear requiring more than level 30 cannot be equipped at the current cap. Classic level-60 pre-BiS lists are not beta evidence.
