Dun Morogh rares: Ghostfang and Classic spawn references

Rare-mob hunt references, loot and respawn windows.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

Where does Ghostfang spawn in WoW Forever?

Ghostfang is a reported level-10 tameable rare cat in Dun Morogh. Two Forever community tables list four waypoint leads: 74.2 / 63.2, 79.4 / 42.8, 80.4 / 46.6 and 81.6 / 55.4. They are zone-map percentages, not four verified simultaneous spawns. The third point has been corrected from an older imprecise 80.4 / 47.0 reference. Inspect Ghostfang's sourced points on our rare map.

Ghostfang's Steps (item 286751), uncommon cloth boots (item level 10, required level 5), are associated with Ghostfang in the 60.tools Forever rare table and documented as an item in its item snapshot. However Forever Companion has no confirmed loot table for this NPC. This boss–item association remains disputed and is not promoted to a verified drop or percentage. The six creatures in the table below are separate historical Classic references.

Are these respawn times and loot tables reliable for Forever?

No guarantees. Windows come from historical reports and Classic comments; server rules can differ. Loot listings are research references, and a blank cell does not imply an empty drop table. A Classic percentage is not a confirmed Forever beta rate.
