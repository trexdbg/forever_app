Duskstalker in Teldrassil: spawn and Forever loot

A low-level rare mob documented by the site catalogue.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

Where is Duskstalker?

Duskstalker is indexed at level 9 in Teldrassil. The first reference mark is 58.1 / 76.6 on its zone map. There may be other points; see the Rare mobs atlas.

Which Forever loot is listed?

The dataset records Duskstalker's Hide as a Forever-sourced item reference. Any observed rate in community archives is not a guaranteed live drop chance.

What is the respawn timer?

Historical intervals should be treated as estimates. Our catalogue does not have an official beta server timer; check the available points and current build rather than treating the reference as live tracking.
