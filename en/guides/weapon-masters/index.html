WoW Forever weapon masters: weapons and city locations

Which weapons each master teaches, where to look and how to interpret Classic reference locations.

References reviewed: 22 October 2026

Quick answer

In WoW Classic reference data, learning a new weapon type requires visiting the appropriate weapon master. Our directory includes 8 reference weapon masters along with their weapon skills, factions, cities and zone coordinates. These locations and skills are not directly verified on WoW Forever.

Which master teaches polearms or bows?

For polearms, start with Woo Ping in Stormwind (Alliance) or Archibald in Undercity (Horde). A WoW Forever community weapon-trainer guide places polearm training at level 20; this requirement is documentary guidance, not a first-hand trainer check. For bows, the reference list points to Ilyenia Moonfire in Darnassus or Hanashi and Sayoc in Orgrimmar. Open the interactive weapon-master list for every skill.

How do I locate a weapon master?

Open Professions — Weapon Masters and filter by weapon, faction and zone. X/Y coordinates are percentages of the Forever zone map, not GPS. Stormwind was remapped in Forever: Woo Ping's old Classic marker (57.1 / 57.7) no longer lines up with the expanded map; look for him around 63.9 / 69.1 at Weller's Arsenal. In Ironforge, Buliwyf (61.2 / 89.5) and Bixi (62.2 / 89.6) have separate positions. These are published reference waypoints, not direct verification in-game.

Can every class learn every weapon?

No. Weapon eligibility depends on class restrictions and the current game rules. This table describes the masters’ skills in Classic references, not a promise that every character can train all these weapons on Forever.
