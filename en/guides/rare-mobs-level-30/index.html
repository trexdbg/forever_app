Reference guide for the level-30 beta.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

How many rares are referenced up to level 30?

How should you look for them?

Pick a level-appropriate zone, check every point on the rare map and read its provenance. A Classic entry does not confirm a Forever spawn; a client loot entry may exist without a verified in-game drop.
