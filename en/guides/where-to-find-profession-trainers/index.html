Editorial review: 10 October 2026

How do you find the right trainer?

First select a skill on the trainer directory, then filter faction and rank. Map markers require documented coordinates; an area name without an exact position is no excuse to invent a waypoint.

Are old Classic trainers always valid?

No. Some NPC or rank references come from Classic while other trainers are new Forever beta records. Check the precise profile and its provenance before traveling.
