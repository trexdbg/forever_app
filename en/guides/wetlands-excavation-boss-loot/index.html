Where is the entrance and what level is it?

Excavation Site: Wetlands has a reference level bracket of 26–31 in Wetlands. New hill southeast of Whelgar's Excavation Site. Forever adds paths uphill. The beta cap is 30; a database listing does not guarantee every encounter is already playable.
