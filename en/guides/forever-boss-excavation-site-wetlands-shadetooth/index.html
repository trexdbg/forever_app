Where is Shadetooth?

Shadetooth is a documented beta encounter in Excavation Site: Wetlands, a dungeon in Wetlands with a reference bracket of 26–31. New hill southeast of Whelgar's Excavation Site. Forever adds paths uphill. The dungeon zone is known, but no precise floor-map coordinates are invented.
