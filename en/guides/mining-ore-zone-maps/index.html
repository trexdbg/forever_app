Where to mine ores in WoW Forever?

Locate documented mining sites without treating potential nodes as live spawns.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

Which ore types are on the atlas?

The 10 maps contain 1749 reference marks. Recorded minerals include Thorium riche (skill 275), Petit thorium (skill 245), Vrai-argent (skill 230), Sombrefer (skill 230), Argent stellaire (skill 245), Thorium hakkari (skill 275). Mining skill is not character level.

Will every pin contain ore?

No. GatherLite references possible locations, not simultaneous live nodes. Higher-level areas may also be dangerous for a level-30 beta character. Open the mining maps to filter veins and zoom.
