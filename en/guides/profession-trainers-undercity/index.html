Where exactly are they located?

These records include the city but do not contain exact X/Y map coordinates. We do not invent waypoints. Open Profession trainers and maps to use the currently sourced map pins, profession filters and faction filters.

Which ranks can the trainers teach?

The names Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert and Artisan describe profession-training tiers, not a guaranteed character-level unlock. A listed rank does not confirm that the option is unlocked on the current beta build.

What should you check before travelling?

Choose the appropriate profession and training tier, confirm your faction, and verify the service in-game. Classic trainer assignments and Forever beta restrictions may vary. Some entries are retained as guide references pending direct testing.
