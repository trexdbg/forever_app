Where is The Baron?

The Baron is a documented beta encounter in Ruins of Lordaeron, a dungeon in Tirisfal Glades with a reference bracket of 15–20. Inside the Ruins of Lordaeron above Undercity. Locate the instance portal in the ruined city. The dungeon zone is known, but no precise floor-map coordinates are invented.
