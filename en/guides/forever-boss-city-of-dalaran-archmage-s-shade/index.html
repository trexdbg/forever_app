Is Shade of the Archmage the same as Archmage's Shade?

Archmage's Shade is the encounter name on this page; Shade of the Archmage is wording also used in our equipment references. The encounter is documented in City of Dalaran (Alterac Mountains), with a 28–33 indicative bracket and 4 reported reward leads. These are beta references, not verified drop rates. Compare the level-30 gear guide.

Where is Archmage's Shade?

Archmage's Shade is a documented beta encounter in City of Dalaran, a dungeon in Alterac Mountains with a reference bracket of 28–33. Enter through Dalaran sewers. A questline grants the sewer key; check entry requirements. The dungeon zone is known, but no precise floor-map coordinates are invented.

Use the full dungeon and boss atlas to plan your run.

What are the reported fight mechanics?

Final violet chamber; avoid orbs and group-wide polymorph. This is beta community guide guidance, not a guarantee that abilities and phases have remained unchanged.

Can a level-30 beta character run this encounter?

The reference dungeon bracket is 28–33, while the current beta character cap is level 30. This bracket is guidance, not a certified minimum entry level. Blizzard officially opened City of Dalaran for testing on 8 October 2026; check current access requirements and its sewer-key questline.
