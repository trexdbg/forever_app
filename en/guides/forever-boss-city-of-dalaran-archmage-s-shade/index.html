Where is Shade of the Archmage?

The encounter (also called Archmage's Shade) is the eighth and final boss of City of Dalaran, in Alterac Mountains. Enter via the Dalaran sewer entrance; a Dalaran Sewers Key is required and the relevant quest chain differs by faction. The Method dungeon guide describes Horde and Alliance attunements and access to the Purple Parlor through the dungeon. No precise indoor coordinates are claimed here.

How do you defeat Shade of the Archmage?

The Forever encounter journal describes Arcane Bolt on the tank, Bounding Mana projectiles, Arcane Explosion within roughly 20 yards and Mass Polymorph followed by Evocation when the boss runs out of mana. Keep ranged players out of the explosion radius and move clear of the returning mana orb. Check current mechanics in-game; these are source-based tactics, not a verified live combat capture.

How reliable is this loot list?

Documented Forever ≠ personally confirmed. The item ID, boss association and source URL are documented, but observed percentages come from other players' reports. Do not confuse these with October beta-guide estimates. The earlier data for three equipment drops was already linked by a separate Forever boss-specific community table; new evidence adds a quest item and the blueprint to that documented set.
