Are the respawn times accurate in Forever?

No guarantee. They come from historical Classic references, and actual beta timers, spawn points and roaming behavior can differ. Multiple marks never guarantee simultaneous spawns.

Are these complete Forever loot tables?

No: only documented notable items are shown. Classic drop records and percentages must not be treated as verified Forever drop rates, and missing loot entries do not prove an empty table.
