What ingredients do WoW Forever potions need?

A curated set of Forever crafting spell references with the profession and ingredients required for each recipe.

References reviewed: 10 October 2026

Quick answer: what ingredients does a potion need?

Potions are made by characters with the Alchemy profession who have learned the appropriate recipe. Exact reagents can differ between game versions. The list below follows Wowhead Forever crafting spell records, rather than current retail WoW, and also includes two quest-related Engineering and Blacksmithing crafts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a Healing Potion?

Alchemy: 1 Bruiseweed + 1 Briarthorn + 1 Leaded Vial. One Healing Potion is used in the Badlands quest “Liquid Stone”.

How do I make a Lesser Invisibility Potion?

Alchemy: 1 Fadeleaf + 1 Wild Steelbloom + 1 Leaded Vial. “Liquid Stone” also requires one.

How do I craft Frost Oil?

4 Khadgar's Whisker + 2 Wintersbite + 1 Leaded Vial. Classic records list Bro'kin as selling the recipe, not necessarily the finished oil or any live Forever stock.

Can another player craft these items?

Yes, where the recipe is available on the server and the output is tradeable. Bring the reagents to a crafter or look for the finished item on the auction house. Player availability and prices are not tracked in real time.

What this data does not prove

A recipe in a database does not prove that players can currently learn or craft it in game. Names, reagents and output quantities may change between Forever builds. Attributed reusable data (JSON) · Methodology.
