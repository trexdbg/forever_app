Quick answer: which profession makes a potion in Forever?

There is a key beta change: healing potions are crafted using First Aid, not the old Alchemy recipes. Mana and utility potions remain Alchemy crafts in the referenced spells. The table below separates those sources and shows the precise reagents and output per craft. Older Classic spell IDs may still exist in databases without being learnable on Forever.

Reference: Forever First Aid profession guide ↗.

Are healing potions still made with Alchemy?

Forever beta's new healing potion recipes use First Aid rather than Alchemy. Old Alchemy spell records are still visible but do not prove that the old recipe can be learned in the beta. Forever First Aid guide ↗

Where do I buy vials and spices?

The Forever First Aid guide directs players to Trade Suppliers for vials and spices; live stock and individual vendors remain unverified. Forever First Aid guide ↗

Why does a recipe make two or three items?

Crafting output differs from reagent quantities. The Gyrochronatom and several potions yield two items, while Frost Oil yields three according to their Forever spells. Forever First Aid guide ↗

Can every recipe be used before level 30?

No. A beta spell record does not prove that it is trainable or usable before the level-30 cap. Check skill and character-level requirements on the linked source. Forever First Aid guide ↗

What this data does not prove

A recipe in a database does not prove that players can currently learn or craft it in game. Names, reagents and output quantities may change between Forever builds. Attributed reusable data (JSON) · Methodology.
