Fizzlefuse: Horde Engineering vendor location and schematics

Find this vendor and check documented item listings.

Editorial review: 11 October 2026

Where can you find Fizzlefuse?

Fizzlefuse is the Horde Engineering merchant at 49.8 / 29.6, west of the Crossroads in The Barrens. Two independent Forever community guides agree on this zone-map waypoint: 60.tools Merchant's Favor and WoW Professions. It is a community-documented location, not a live gameplay capture; the NPC's numeric ID remains unknown. View Fizzlefuse on our interactive vendor map.
