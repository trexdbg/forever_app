Beta & dungeons

Which WoW Forever dungeons are open in the beta?

Blizzard's published availability list, separate from future dungeon announcements.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

What is the current cap?

Blizzard's 1 October 2026 development notes raised the beta cap to level 30 and listed dungeon access including Excavation Site: Wetlands, Razorfen Downs (25+) and Uldaman (30+). On 8 October, the City of Dalaran dungeon opened for testing.

What about upcoming dungeons?

An entry in the Dungeons atlas does not automatically mean it is open. Planned instances are documented separately from currently testable ones. Use level-bracket gear to check item requirements.
