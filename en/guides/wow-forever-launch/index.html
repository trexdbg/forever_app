WoW Forever launch: date, time and useful day-one tools

A practical launch checklist linking to Forever references while keeping Classic quest routes clearly identified.

Published 3 November 2026

When does WoW Forever launch?

Blizzard announced a global launch on November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST, equivalent to November 5 at midnight in Paris. Access is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or game time; optional upgrade editions are not required. Check the official Blizzard announcement for any last-minute schedule changes.

What should I prepare for day one?

Start with your character's talent trees and class choices. Then use the profession trainers and vendor directory to find reference locations. Browse rare mobs and dungeons as you explore. The mining maps are community waypoint references, not guaranteed live resource spawns.

Are ForEveroth quest routes confirmed for Forever?

No. The public Quests module currently contains WoW Classic routes. It is useful for orientation, but Forever can change NPCs, quests, rewards and travel routes. Check the live in-game quest log. Do not interpret these historical steps as verified Forever leveling routes.

Will beta loot and item records still apply at launch?

Not necessarily. A datamined item, historical Classic waypoint or beta-reported boss drop is not proof of live availability on launch servers. Our leveling gear references help you compare options while preserving source distinctions. A listed item does not imply a confirmed drop rate, location or vendor stock. Read the data methodology for more detail.

A quick launch checklist

  • Review your class, weapon restrictions and talent options.
  • Confirm trainers, vendors and quest objectives in the actual game before traveling.
  • Treat beta records and Classic references as starting points, not guaranteed live facts.
  • Bookmark the Forever guide library and interactive tools for quick lookup.

Launch date source: Blizzard — World of Warcraft: Forever. Prepared before launch; this page does not claim direct observations of live launch servers.
