WoW Forever beginner guide: what to do after launch

A practical path from choosing talents to finding trainers, gearing up and exploring. Forever and Classic references stay clearly separated.

Published 3 November · Updated 17 November 2026

Has WoW Forever launched?

Yes: the announced global launch was November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. Pacific time (November 5 at midnight in Paris). Blizzard says access is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or game time; optional editions are not required. Official Blizzard announcement. This is a getting-started guide, not a live server-status monitor.

What should I do first in WoW Forever?

  1. Plan your class: use the nine class talent calculators and check the three Legacy trees (16 points maximum).
  2. Find your trainers: use the profession trainer map and weapon-master filters to locate relevant reference waypoints.
  3. Review your gear: compare level-based BiS suggestions and check whether each source is documented for Forever or only Classic.
  4. Explore: browse rare mobs, dungeons and bosses, and mining maps as guides, never guaranteed live spawns.
  5. Save useful tools: bookmark trainers locally or share a Legacy talent build without an account.

Where do I train a different weapon skill?

Different weapon masters teach different weapon types, depending on city and faction. Jump directly to staff trainers, polearm trainers or sword trainers. The map distinguishes documented Forever reference waypoints from historical Classic locations; it does not imply every trainer was checked firsthand in live gameplay.

Are the quest routes on ForEveroth confirmed for Forever?

No. The Quests module still exposes WoW Classic routes. Those routes remain public and useful for orientation but are not validated Forever leveling paths. NPCs, ferry travel, objectives and rewards can differ. Check the in-game journal. For a specific documented Forever quest, see The Wrath of Rath'mael, which separates client references from player-reported prerequisites.

Does a Forever item listing prove it is obtainable?

No. A datamined item, a community loot association or a Classic waypoint is not proof of live availability, drop probability, vendor inventory or active spawn. We preserve those provenance distinctions in item and boss references; unknown values stay unknown. Read our sourcing policy.
