Hall of Thanes: entrance, bosses and loot

Entrance guidance and documented loot for Hall of Thanes.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

Where is the entrance and what level is it?

Hall of Thanes has a reference level bracket of 13–18 in Ironforge. Beneath the High Seat. Take the Old Ironforge passage on the left and descend to the portal. The beta cap is 30; a database listing does not guarantee every encounter is already playable.
