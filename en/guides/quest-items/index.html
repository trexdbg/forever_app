Where to get items required for quests

Vendors, crafters, reagents and references — with a clear distinction between Classic and Forever data.

References reviewed: 10 October 2026

Quick answer: where can I get quest items?

First determine whether the item is a monster drop, vendor stock, a crafted item or an item traded by other players. A Classic vendor might sell a recipe rather than the finished product. For crafted items, check the reagents and ask a crafter or use the auction house.

A documented example is the quest series in the Badlands. Wowhead Forever lists the following requirements, but ForEveroth does not confirm that these objectives or vendors are active in the live game.

Example: buying or crafting a Gyrochronatom

“Gyro... What?” needs 1 Gyrochronatom. Classic vendor references in our catalog include Sovik in Orgrimmar (75.6 / 25.2), Gearcutter Cogspinner in Ironforge (68.2 / 44.2) and Jinky Twizzlefixxit in Thousand Needles (77.6 / 77.8). Stock is limited and these locations are not confirmed for Forever. Engineers can also craft it using 1 Iron Bar + 1 Gold Power Core. Read the vendor guide.

Example: is Frost Oil sold by a vendor?

Find the Frost Oil recipe in vendor references ↗

The recipe and the finished item are different. In Classic references, Bro'kin sells the Frost Oil recipe (Alterac Mountains, approximately 38.2 / 38.8), but the quest asks for the crafted Frost Oil. An alchemist can make it with 4 Khadgar's Whisker, 2 Wintersbite and 1 Leaded Vial. See the full materials catalog.

Frequently asked quest-item questions

Where can I get Gyrochronatom for a quest?

Gyrochronatom: Engineering. Reagents: 1 × Iron Bar · 1 × Gold Power Core. Reference yield: 2. Some Classic vendors have limited supply; Forever stock is not verified. Forever source ↗

Where can I get Frost Oil for a quest?

Frost Oil: Alchemy. Reagents: 4 × Khadgar's Whisker · 2 × Wintersbite · 1 × Leaded Vial. Reference yield: 3. Forever source ↗

Where can I get Healing Potion for a quest?

Healing Potion: First Aid. Reagents: 1 × Leaded Vial · 1 × Hot Spices · 1 × Bruiseweed. Reference yield: 2. Forever source ↗

Where can I get Lesser Invisibility Potion for a quest?

Lesser Invisibility Potion: Alchemy. Reagents: 1 × Fadeleaf · 1 × Wild Steelbloom · 1 × Leaded Vial. Reference yield: 2. Forever source ↗

Where can I get Patterned Bronze Bracers for a quest?

Patterned Bronze Bracers: Blacksmithing. Reagents: 5 × Bronze Bar · 2 × Coarse Grinding Stone. Reference yield: 2. Forever source ↗

Does a vendor sell the finished item or just its recipe?

It depends on the vendor. Classic sources list Bro'kin as selling the Frost Oil recipe, not guaranteed finished Frost Oil. Vendor map ↗
