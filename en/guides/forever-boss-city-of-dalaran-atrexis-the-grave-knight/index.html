Where is Atrexis the Grave Knight?

Atrexis the Grave Knight is a documented beta encounter in City of Dalaran, a dungeon in Alterac Mountains with a reference bracket of 28–33. Enter through Dalaran sewers. A questline grants the sewer key; check entry requirements. The dungeon zone is known, but no precise floor-map coordinates are invented.

What are the reported fight mechanics?

Ritual circle in sewers; rising skeletons and disarm. This is beta community guide guidance, not a guarantee that abilities and phases have remained unchanged.
