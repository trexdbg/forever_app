What does a Favor cost mean?

The displayed amount is a Forever database reference, not a live merchant inventory readout. Friendly and Honored recipes can require reputation independently from profession skill.

Where do you find sellers?

Open Vendors and filter documented Forever sales to inspect seller, faction and item. A catalogue listing does not establish that the item is currently in stock.
