City of Dalaran: entrance, bosses and loot

Entrance guidance and documented loot for City of Dalaran.

Editorial review: 16 October 2026

Where is the entrance and what level is it?

City of Dalaran has a reference level bracket of 28–33 in Alterac Mountains. Enter through Dalaran sewers. A questline grants the sewer key; check entry requirements. The beta cap is 30; a database listing does not guarantee every encounter is already playable.
