Where to buy a Bronze Tube

Reference vendors, factions and map coordinates — without claiming to know live WoW Forever inventories.

Reference reviewed : 9 October 2026

Quick answer

The Bronze Tube (item 4371) is an Engineering component. Classic reference data lists vendors including Sovik (Horde, Orgrimmar), Gearcutter Cogspinner (Alliance, Ironforge), and neutral vendors. The Classic dataset marks this item as limited supply. ForEveroth does not have real-time WoW Forever vendor stock data.

Why might a vendor not have it in stock?

Limited-supply items may be missing from a vendor’s inventory at a given moment. Historical comments and estimated waits do not establish a guaranteed restock timer, especially on a server that may differ from Classic.
