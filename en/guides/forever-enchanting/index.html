Enchanting in WoW Forever: recipes and trainers

Changes to Enchanting and where to train.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

What changed for Enchanting?

The beta client diff for build 1.60.1.70291 dated 2026-10-08 lists 261 entries for Enchanting, including 63 newly identified entries compared with Classic. These are client-data entries, not proof of recipes craftable before the level-30 cap.

Example archive titles: Idol of Swiftness ; Dust to Motes. Unverified localizations remain in English.

How can you obtain a particular recipe?

Check the trainer tier and skill, then search for the pattern in Vendors. A datamined record is not necessarily sold or learnable. Crafting difficulty colors do not equal learning prerequisites.
