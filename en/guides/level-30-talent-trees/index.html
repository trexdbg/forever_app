WoW Forever talents: planning a level-30 build

Plan talent points using beta references rather than an old Classic template.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

Are Forever talents the same as Classic?

The calculator represents 9 classes and 27 beta talent trees, including prerequisites and ranks. An older Classic allocation may no longer work after Forever changes.

How can you test a level-30 build?

Select the class, set the level to 30 and spend points. Shareable links make it easy to compare, but a build's value depends on your role, equipment and client build.
