Ruins of Lordaeron: entrance, bosses and loot

Entrance guidance and documented loot for Ruins of Lordaeron.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

Where is the entrance and what level is it?

Ruins of Lordaeron has a reference level bracket of 15–20 in Tirisfal Glades. Inside the Ruins of Lordaeron above Undercity. Locate the instance portal in the ruined city. The beta cap is 30; a database listing does not guarantee every encounter is already playable.
