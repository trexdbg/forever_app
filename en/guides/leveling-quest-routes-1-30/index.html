WoW Forever quest leveling routes 1–30

Follow the quest timeline and zone maps.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

How do you follow a quest route?

In the Quest guide, select faction and race, inspect the step's level and move through the route. Panels show objectives and quest hand-ins; X/Y pins belong to the displayed zone map.

Are Classic quests identical in Forever?

Not guaranteed. Routes come from Vanilla Questing/Classic. Forever may move quest givers, change rewards or add quests. Follow the actual in-game journal rather than treating the historical route as a beta confirmation.

Is progress saved?

The Next control changes the step without maintaining permanent quest-completion history. Step links remain shareable.
