Can you farm these deposits during the level-30 beta?

Do not assume so. This is advanced mining material and primarily a future-progression reference. With the Forever beta character cap currently at level 30, high-level zone access and profession requirements cannot be presumed achievable. We retain these source points for later phases and preparation.

Do several dots represent simultaneous mining spawns?

No. Many deposits share spawn pools or may appear in different positions between respawns. A coordinate is not a promise of a node. Avoid using unverified old Classic node probabilities as confirmed Forever spawn rates.
