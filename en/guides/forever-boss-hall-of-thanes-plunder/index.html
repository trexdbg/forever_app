Plunder: boss tactics and loot in Hall of Thanes

Reported encounter and rewards from the Hall of Thanes beta dungeon.

Reviewed 10 October 2026

Where is Plunder?

Plunder is a documented beta encounter in Hall of Thanes, a dungeon in Ironforge with a reference bracket of 13–18. Beneath the High Seat. Take the Old Ironforge passage on the left and descend to the portal. The dungeon zone is known, but no precise floor-map coordinates are invented.

Use the full dungeon and boss atlas to plan your run.

What are the reported fight mechanics?

Knockback: fight with your back to a wall, away from trash. This is beta community guide guidance, not a guarantee that abilities and phases have remained unchanged.
