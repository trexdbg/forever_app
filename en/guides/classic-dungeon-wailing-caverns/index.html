Wailing Caverns in Forever: boss loot and beta changes

Check reported changes without confusing Classic loot with Forever beta drops.

Editorial review: 10 October 2026

What changed in Wailing Caverns?

This returning Classic dungeon has a reference level bracket of 17–24 in The Barrens. Our local beta references list the following changes:

  • Five-piece Embrace of the Viper set has updated bonuses in Forever; Druid snake transformation is reported.
  • Some displayed source drop rates are Classic-based and must not be presented as Forever rates.

These are attributed beta sources, not guarantees that every listed reward currently drops.
